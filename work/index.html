ExamCompanion turns uploaded learning material into summaries, flashcards, questions, and contextual explanations. The engineering challenge was not only generation: ingestion, background work, retrieval, payments, authentication, persistence, and production operations all had to fit together.

ROLEBackend, AI & DevOps Engineer / Team Lead
TEAMLed 4 developers
CONSTRAINTMultiple source formats + async processing + grounded AI
OUTCOME3,000+ MAU / production launch in 6 months
Engineering decisions
  • Separated ingestion and generation from request/response paths with asynchronous processing
  • Used PostgreSQL plus vector retrieval to keep generated answers grounded in each learner's source material
  • Persisted generated study materials so users could retrieve prior results instead of regenerating unnecessarily
  • Integrated subscriptions, Stripe and Paystack flows with authenticated account state
  • Operated application components on AWS/Kubernetes with health checks, rolling updates, scaling, metrics, and availability monitoring
  • Documented backend behavior and coordinated delivery across a four-person engineering team
