A little context

The visible interface is only one part of a product. I like working on the systems underneath it: the APIs, data flows, integrations, and infrastructure that make the experience feel fast and dependable.

I have built backend systems for products across AI learning, event management and ticketing, and creator marketing. EventTally included event, vendor, ticket, payment, and collaborator workflows, alongside the integrations that connect product actions to reliable data.

These projects included authentication hardening, Google OAuth, and GitHub Actions CI/CD, contributing to a 13% reduction in deployment time while supporting 99.99% uptime.

I care about robust systems, clear communication, and learning whatever the next problem requires. My goal is to build software that is useful today and dependable as it grows.
