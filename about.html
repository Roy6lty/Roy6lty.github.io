01 / CONTEXT

A little background

I became interested in DevOps because I want to understand the whole path from a request to a running service.

I have built backend systems for AI learning, event management and ticketing, and creator marketing. ExamCompanion reached 3,000+ monthly active users and more than $1,000 in revenue. EventTally has supported more than 100 events across Africa.

For ExamCompanion, I led a team of four through six months of work from conception to launch. That experience made the connection between product decisions, engineering trade-offs, delivery, and operations very clear.

I love networking because it makes those ideas visible. Docker labs and Kubernetes exercises let me test routing, isolation, service discovery, deployments, and failure instead of only reading about them.
