Why this series exists

Do not memorise the command.
Understand the system.

I learn infrastructure by changing a working topology and watching what breaks. That makes subnets, gateways, routing, service discovery, deployment, and recovery concrete.

The series will grow with the system. Every new entry adds one concept, records what changed, and explains what I learned before moving on.
